IMPORTANT SETUP CHECK
The objective activity names must match ScienceActivityType exactly.
The spacecraft propulsionAvailable must be at least propulsionRequired + minimumPropulsionReserve.
The total instrument dataOutputMb must not exceed requiredDataStorageMb.
